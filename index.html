Critical Rule
Always use realized price incl. buyer's premium ÷ carat weight for PPC. Hammer price underestimates by 12–25%.
Certification Filter
Only GRS, Gübelin, SSEF, or GIA certificates are auction-recognized for origin & treatment attribution.

Data Normalization Methodology

01
Price per Carat Formula
  • Realized Price (incl. buyer's premium) ÷ Carat Weight
  • Lock currency to HKMA daily FX on auction date
  • All values reported in USD throughout this presentation
02
Missing Data Imputation
  • Flag lots without lab cert as treatment-unknown
  • Use median PPC by origin × carat bracket for comparable imputation
03
Outlier Handling
  • Cap extreme outliers so one exceptional lot does not distort the whole peer group
  • World-record lots are tagged separately
  • They distort cohort medians if included in standard analysis
04
Pass Rate Signal
  • Track % of lots sold per auction session
  • Pass rate >40% = buyer's market → sourcing window (buy signal)
  • Pass rate <10% = seller's market → exit window (sell signal)

ORIGIN & TREATMENT ANALYSIS

Key Insights

Origin Premium
Ruby Origin Stack
  • Burma (Mogok) — +50–200% over Mozambique at equivalent quality
  • Mozambique (MRM) — base reference; ~80% of world supply
  • Gap narrowing — Estrela de Fura ($34.8M) proved Mozambican origin no longer a ceiling
  • Burma sanctions accelerating perception normalization
Sapphire Origin Stack
  • Kashmir — +300–700% over Ceylon (no supply since 1930s)
  • Burma — +80–200% over Ceylon
  • Ceylon (Sri Lanka) — base reference; most available
  • Madagascar — 10–30% discount to Ceylon for heated stones
Treatment Premium
Ruby Treatment
  • ~95% of rubies are heated — unheated is structurally rare
  • Unheated commands +50–100% premium over heated equivalents
  • GRS "No indications of heating" = highest certification tier
  • Only GRS, Gübelin, SSEF, GIA accepted at major houses
Sapphire Treatment
  • Unheated sapphire: +20–300% over heated equivalents
  • Kashmir stones almost never heated — already excellent color
  • Ceylon heated = mainstream; unheated Ceylon = collector tier
